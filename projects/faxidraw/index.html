FaxiDraw - Foldable Drawing Plotter

Status: archived · Published Jul 1, 2017

  • plotter
  • open hardware
  • 3d printing
  • motion control
  • cnc

FaxiDraw is a foldable drawing plotter I built around 3D-printed parts, open hardware, and a very simple motivation: I wanted a machine that could turn digital paths into real pen movement without needing a large CNC-style setup.

It is one of those projects that looks playful from the outside, but quickly becomes very technical once you start tuning it. A plotter needs smooth motion, enough rigidity, repeatable positioning, and mechanical parts that do not flex too much when the pen touches the paper.

What I like about FaxiDraw is that it sits somewhere between art tool and engineering exercise. It is a machine built to explore motion, fabrication, and the satisfaction of watching code become a physical trace on paper.
