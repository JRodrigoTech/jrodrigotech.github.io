3D-Printed Things

Status: active · Published Apr 30, 2014

  • 3d printing
  • mechanical design
  • fabrication
  • open source
  • cad

3D-Printed Things is my small archive of printable parts, mechanical experiments, and useful workshop objects. It is not a single polished product; it is more like a drawer full of digital parts that have been useful at some point.

A lot of my projects end up needing some printed support piece: a bracket, a holder, an enclosure, an adapter, a test fit, or a quick mechanical part to check an idea.

I keep this collection because those small parts tell part of the story behind the bigger projects. Electronics rarely live as bare PCBs forever.

This repository is the messy-but-useful side of fabrication: practical, iterative, and very tied to the bench.
