3D-Printed Things

Status: active · Published Apr 30, 2014

  • 3d printing
  • mechanical design
  • fabrication
  • open source
  • cad

3D-Printed Things is my small archive of printable parts, mechanical experiments, and useful workshop objects. It is not a single polished product; it is more like a drawer full of digital parts that have been useful at some point.

A lot of my projects end up needing some printed support piece: a bracket, a holder, an enclosure, an adapter, a test fit, or a quick mechanical part to check an idea. 3D printing is very good for that kind of work because it lets me test something physical without turning every small detail into a manufacturing problem.

I keep this collection because those small parts tell part of the story behind the bigger projects. Electronics rarely live as bare PCBs forever. They need cases, mounts, spacers, jigs, fixtures, and strange little pieces that only make sense once you are actually building the thing.

This repository is the messy-but-useful side of fabrication: practical, iterative, and very tied to the bench. Some parts are experiments, some are utilities, and some are just there because they solved a problem once and may solve it again later.
