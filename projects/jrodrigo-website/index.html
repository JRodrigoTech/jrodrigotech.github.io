This website is my public home base for projects, notes, services, and technical documentation. I do not want it to be only a polished portfolio; I want it to feel more like a digital lab where I can organize what I build, what I learn, and what I can help with.

The current version is built around Astro and content files. That makes the site simple to deploy, fast to load, and easy to maintain without turning every update into a full application change. Projects, notes, wiki entries, and pages can live as structured content instead of being hardcoded into templates.

I rebuilt the site because I needed a clearer place to connect several parts of my work: electronics, automation, retro hardware, 3D printing, private engineering projects, and software tools. A normal portfolio layout was too flat for that. I needed something closer to a workshop index.

The site is still evolving, but that is part of the point. It gives me a place to publish finished projects, document useful technical notes, and show the kind of practical engineering work I enjoy doing.
