This website is my public home base for projects, notes, services, and technical documentation. I do not want it to be only a polished portfolio; I want it to feel more like a digital lab.

The current version is built around Astro and content files. That makes the site simple to deploy, fast to load, and easier to maintain without turning every update into a full application change.

I rebuilt the site because I needed a clearer place to connect electronics, automation, retro hardware, 3D printing, private engineering projects, and software tools.

The site is still evolving, but that is part of the point.
